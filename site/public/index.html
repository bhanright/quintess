edit text

Change the words that are already there.

Click a line or a paragraph and type. Paragraphs re-wrap at their own width and alignment, bold and italic carry through, and new letters are drawn in the document's own font when it has them. A preview marks every line that will move before you apply.

    §05

    Questions.

    p. 06
    Is it really free?

    Yes. Quintess is open source under MIT or Apache-2.0. There is no paid tier, no trial and no ads.

    Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

    The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about it. Choose More info, then Run anyway. To check the download first, compare its SHA-256 with the one published beside it on the release page; in PowerShell, run Get-FileHash .\Quintess-*-setup.exe.

    Does it work on Mac or Linux?

    Not yet. Quintess is built and tested for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The engine is portable Rust, so other systems are possible later.

    Can it really edit the text in any PDF?

    It edits real text, line by line or a paragraph at a time, keeping the fonts and styles where it can. Scanned pages are pictures, so they need OCR first, and a page drawn as outlines has no text to edit.

    How do updates work?

    On first launch, Quintess asks whether to check GitHub for new versions. If you say yes, it checks at most once a day and asks before downloading anything. You can also check by hand from the Help menu.

    Does it need administrator rights?

    No. It installs for your user account only. It can offer itself for opening PDFs without taking over your current default.
