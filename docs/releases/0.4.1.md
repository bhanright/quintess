Quintess 0.4.1 fixes two things that didn't respond.

## Fixes

- **Closing a tab with its ×** works again in the Studio look. Clicking the × used to do nothing.
- **Enter in Go to page** now goes to the page, so you no longer have to click Go. Pressing Enter is also more reliable in Find, the password prompts, the sidebar search and the certificate password box.
