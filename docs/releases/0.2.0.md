Quintess 0.2.0 adds a Linux release, and many new tools for editing, checking and producing PDFs. Everything still runs on your computer, with no account.

## Downloads

- **Windows 10/11 (x86_64):** `Quintess-0.2.0-setup.exe`. Installs for your user account, with no administrator rights. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.
- **Linux (x86_64):** `quintess_0.2.0_amd64.deb` for Ubuntu and Debian, or `quintess-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` with an install script for other distributions. See [Installing on Linux](https://quintess.cc/guide/#installing-on-linux).

Each file has a `.sha256` checksum beside it.

## New

**Editing**
- Spell check as you type in page text, comments and form fields, with a right-click menu and an F7 panel. Nine dictionaries are included; about 55 more download in the app.
- Edit pictures and shapes already on the page: move, resize, rotate, flip, crop, replace, delete and restack, with opacity and blend modes.
- Text wrap: paragraphs flow around a picture and follow it when it moves or is resized.
- A Layers panel: show, hide, lock, rename, reorder, create and delete layers, and move objects between them.
- Measuring: distance, perimeter and area at a scale you set or calibrate, saved as standard measurement comments.
- Read Out Loud with the system's voices, using Acrobat's shortcuts.

**Signing and security**
- Sign with a certificate file (.pfx), a certificate installed in Windows, or an identity made in Quintess; add a trusted timestamp and long-term validation.
- Signature checks explain the result in plain language, show the certificate chain, and can ask the issuers about revocation.
- Encrypt a document for specific people's certificates, each with their own permissions, and open files encrypted for you.

**Forms**
- Forms made in Acrobat calculate totals, check entries and format amounts and dates as you fill them, including as you type. Form scripts run in a sandbox with no file or network access.
- XFDF import and export keep nested fields, list boxes with several choices and styled text.

**Creating and exporting**
- Create PDFs from Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, HTML, Markdown and web pages. Built-in converters cover Word and spreadsheets; LibreOffice, Microsoft Office or a browser are used when installed, and Quintess says which converter ran.
- Scan to PDF (WIA on Windows, SANE on Linux), with OCR.
- Export tables to Excel or CSV, and pages to PowerPoint slides.
- Portfolios: one PDF holding several files of any type.
- List, play and save embedded audio, video and 3D; place a video on a page.

**Printing and standards**
- Booklets (with signatures, creep and gutter) and posters tiled onto smaller paper.
- Output preview: the inks a document uses, one plate at a time, and total ink coverage warnings. Convert to grayscale or RGB to CMYK.
- PDF/A 1b, 2b, 2u, 3b, 3u, 4 and 4f, and PDF/X-1a and X-4: check and convert. A file is marked only when nothing unfixable remains.
- Accessibility: declare PDF/UA-1 after the check passes and you have reviewed what needs a person, and edit the tag tree and reading order.

**Speed and memory**
- Large documents open quickly and use less memory: pages are kept within a memory budget you can change in Preferences, and pages ahead are prepared in the direction you scroll.
- OpenGL drawing by default, with a Graphics choice in Preferences; no work while the window is idle.

## Fixed

- Text that mixes Hebrew or Arabic with English words and numbers copies, searches and exports in reading order.
- Article threads are listed, and their text can be copied in order.

## Known limitations

- Encryption for certificates hasn't been tested with Adobe Acrobat yet.
- PDF/A and PDF/X checks are not a certified validator; use veraPDF or your print shop's preflight for a final word.
- Print production and color conversion use simple formulas, without ICC profiles or overprint.
